Welcome

Meet Islamic Beginners Introduction Guide.

  1. Meet Islamic Beginners Introduction Guide.
  2. Learn a few verses at a time, then review them together.

Learn a few verses at a time, mark them remembered when you are ready, and review earlier ones together every few sections.

A session is unhurried. Practise a small group of verses. Mark Remembered today only when you feel ready. The app will not move you on, and it will not mark a group done for you. If nothing is waiting, you can rest.

Everything stays on this device. There is no account and no sign-up.

Learning wudu and prayer: this first release does not include step-by-step prayer guides. Please learn those with a qualified teacher or imam at your local mosque.

This guide is a general beginner's aid, not a religious ruling (fatwa). It has not been reviewed by a scholar. Practice differs between schools of thought (madhhabs), so please learn from a qualified teacher or imam at your local mosque.
